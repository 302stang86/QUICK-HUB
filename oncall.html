How the rotation works

  • First Up is the primary on-call technician. Second Up is the backup if First Up is unavailable.
  • For vacation or personal time, note it and reassign that week's dropdowns. Keep two different techs per week; a duplicate is flagged red.
  • The current week is highlighted in green. Pick a date from the calendar to outline that week in dark red.
  • Weeks containing a U.S. federal holiday are marked with a ★ and the holiday name so techs can plan ahead.
  • The rotation runs continuously from 2026 into 2027, advancing one technician each week.
  • Refer to Fire Defense Equipment Co. policies regarding holiday pay, holiday paid days off, and which holidays are included.

Technician roster

    Roster changes apply to upcoming weeks only. Past and current weeks keep their assignments. Any two techs may share a week as First Up and Second Up. Second Up is never First Up the following week. Managers are listed for reference only and are never placed in the rotation.
