# Fire Defense enemy field guide

The map is now a round arena about 52 units across (radius 26.2), five times the area of the previous 24 × 18 rectangle. Rookie Wobble and every enemy are drawn and collide at half their previous size. Rookie Wobble starts every room at the center. Enemies spawn randomly in the outer ring (beyond half the radius) at any angle, at least three world units from the hero. The camera follows the hero; the minimap shows remaining enemies. Mobile game zoom is limited to 75–125%, with a 100% reset button and double-tap zoom disabled.

## Introduction plan

- Room 1 teaches movement and automatic hose aiming with Ember Creatures.
- Base enemies unlock gradually through Room 30; each new type is guaranteed in its debut room. Previously introduced types enter the random room pool.
- Mini-boss debuts: Rooms 2, 4, 7, 9, 13, 15, 19, 21, 25 and 27. Unlocked mini-bosses can return every third non-boss room.
- Major bosses appear in Rooms 6, 12, 18, 24 and 30. These rooms have fewer base enemies and no mini-boss, giving the player room to read the boss.
- Contact damage uses a 1.1-second grace interval. Speed below is normal world units per second; charges briefly reach 2.7× speed, and pulse movement alternates 0.45× and 1.7× speed. Charging enemies brighten and shake before the rush.
- These are initial balance values. Playtest with both new and upgraded equipment before tuning difficulty or adding more simultaneous enemies.

The roster uses illustrated procedural flame and armored-brute bodies, varied palettes, crests, silhouettes, sizes and movement patterns. HP, damage and speed are distinct for every character.
