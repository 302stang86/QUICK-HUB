Sprinkler System Troubleshooting

FDEC Field Reference — NFPA 13 / 25
System Type
General troubleshooting reference for common configurations. Always follow the manufacturer’s data sheet for the specific valve trim installed and the governing edition of NFPA 13, 25, and 72 adopted by the AHJ. Device behavior and reset order vary by make and model (Reliable, Viking, Victaulic, Tyco, Globe, AGF, and others).
