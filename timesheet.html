Hours Worked

Add a card for each department, customer/job, or leave type. Type/Department and Customer/Job are required on every card. Rate rule: Monday–Friday is Regular (straight time), Saturday is Time & Half (1.5×), Sunday is Double Time (2×). Monday–Friday Regular hours over eight (8) in a day move to Overtime automatically; when a day has more than one card, the day's later card carries the Overtime. Because each weekday caps at eight (8) Regular hours, the Mon–Friday week never exceeds forty (40) Regular hours.
