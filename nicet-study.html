NICET ITM Study Centers

Interactive study guides for the NICET Inspection, Testing & Maintenance of Water-Based Systems (ITWBS) certification, keyed to NFPA 25 (2023). Choose your level to open its study center — dashboards, study guides, an NFPA 25 inspection/testing/maintenance frequency guide, visuals, practice quizzes, and flashcards (including true/false) — or open the Portal Guide for CPD, Work History, and recertification.
