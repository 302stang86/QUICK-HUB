1. Logging in to the NICET Portal

The official candidate portal is hosted at:

https://nicet.useclarus.com/login

Steps to log in

  1. Go to the login page — Open https://nicet.useclarus.com/login in your browser.
  2. Enter your credentials — Use the email address that is on file with NICET and your password.
  3. Forgot password? — Click “Forgot your password? Reset it here.” A reset link will be sent from nicet-noreply@useclarus.com. Check spam/junk and add that address to your safe senders list.
  4. New users — If you have never had a NICET account, use the “fill out a basic profile” / registration link. If you already applied or tested in the past, do not create a new profile — contact NICET instead.
Note: Portal messages and password resets come from nicet-noreply@useclarus.com. Make sure this address is allowed by your email system (especially work email).

After you log in

Your home screen shows your certification cards (each certification/level you hold), expiration timing, and CPD progress. The left-side navigation includes options such as:

  • Manage your CPD (under Continuing Professional Development)
  • Apply (for new or upgrade applications)
  • Your email address (top right) → Profile (for contact info and Work History)

2. What is CPD?

CPD stands for Continuing Professional Development.

NICET uses a points-based system so certificants keep their knowledge and skills current after initial certification. Every certification runs on a three-year (36-month) cycle. To keep a certification in Active status, you must document 90 CPD points for that certification during the three-year period and pay the recertification fee.

Key rule: 90 CPD points are required per certification (per subfield) every three years. Points must come from two or more of the five CPD categories. Each category has a maximum that can count toward the 90.

CPD categories and limits

Category Max points (3 years) % of 90
Active Practitioner 72 (up to 24 per year) 80%
Additional Education 72 80%
Advance Profession 45 50%
Certification Activity 90 100%
Special Exam 45 50%

Full official details: https://www.nicet.org/recertify

3. How to earn CPD points

Active Practitioner (up to 72 points)

You are an active practitioner if your main job regularly involves the technical tasks of your certification area (hands-on, immediate supervisor, or technical project manager).

  • Full-time work that meets the criteria: up to 24 points per year (72 over three years).
  • Minimum threshold for a year: about 1,000 hours or 120 work days (7+ hours each day).
  • Part-time or partial years: use NICET’s formula and round to the nearest whole number. Only totals of 8 or more may be claimed.
  • Required: An up-to-date Work History in the portal must support Active Practitioner claims.

Additional Education (up to 72 points)

Courses, seminars, workshops, distance learning, in-house training, and technical presentations that increase your knowledge in the practice area.

  • Generally 1 CPD point per contact hour (45–60 minute learning period).
  • College courses: 15 points per semester hour or 10 per quarter hour.
  • NICET does not pre-approve courses. The activity must genuinely develop your knowledge, not just review what you already know.
  • Document date(s), location, sponsor/organization, subject, and relevance to your practice area. Certificates of completion are often requested on audit.

Advance Profession (up to 45 points)

Giving back to the profession: committee work, presentations, teaching, professional society membership/meetings, career-day or STEM outreach, NICET subject-matter-expert (SME) participation, etc. Point values vary by activity (see the Recertify page for the full table).

Certification Activity (up to 90 points)

Earning or upgrading a NICET certification (or meeting higher-level exam requirements) during the period can award substantial points — up to the full 90 for an upgrade in the same practice area, or 30 for a related subfield certification.

Special Recertification Exam (45 points)

A special written exam for your practice area and level. Contact NICET (test@nicet.org or 888-476-4238) well before your expiration date if you need this option.

Activities that are relevant to more than one of your certifications can often be counted toward each of those certifications (for example, an 8-hour relevant course can give 8 education points to each applicable subfield).

4. Managing and adding CPD in the portal

You can (and should) enter CPD points as you earn them. The portal keeps a running total and stores your documentation.

How to add CPD credits

  1. Log in at https://nicet.useclarus.com/login.
  2. On the home screen, either:
    • Click “Manage your CPD” in the left navigation under Continuing Professional Development, or
    • On a certification card, click the “90 CPD points” (or similar) link under Renewal Requirements.
  3. Click “Add CPD credits”.
  4. A form opens. Use the dropdown to select a CPD category (Active Practitioner, Additional Education, etc.).
  5. The form expands with fields for points and supporting information. Enter the required details. Descriptions and guidance appear for the selected category.
  6. Upload supporting documentation when available (certificates, agendas, etc.).
  7. Save the entry. Repeat for additional activities.
Active Practitioner: Claims in this category must be backed by an updated Work History. Links to your profile/Work History appear on the home screen, when you add Active Practitioner points, and during checkout.

Tracking progress

Your home screen and certification cards show points claimed so far and time remaining until expiration. Enter points throughout the three-year period so you are ready when the renewal window opens (typically within about 6 months of expiration).

5. Adding and modifying Work History

Work History is stored in your Profile. It is required for Active Practitioner CPD points and for applications/upgrades. Returning candidates (recertification or upgrades) should add only the period since their last submitted work history — do not delete or alter previously submitted entries.

How to open Work History

  1. Log in to the portal.
  2. Click your email address (usually top right).
  3. Choose Profile.
  4. Click the prompt to Update profile.
  5. Scroll down to the Work History section.

You can also reach Work History from links when adding Active Practitioner CPD or during the renewal/checkout flow.

How to add a Work History entry

  1. Click “+ Add”.
  2. Fill in employment details:
    • Employer name
    • Position / title
    • Supervisor name(s)
    • Employment dates
    • Location (city, state)
    • Employment level (full-time / part-time)
  3. At the bottom of the entry, use the “+” control to add Subfield / Technical Area (S/TA) breakouts:
    • Select the S/TA from the dropdown.
    • Enter the percentage of work devoted to that area.
    • Write a detailed description (in your own words) of the work performed in that area.
  4. Percentages for the period must total 100%. Add as many S/TA lines as needed.
  5. Click “+ Add” again if you need another time period or another position (e.g., promotion or change of duties at the same employer).
  6. Save all entries.

Important rules for Work History

  • First-time applicants: Document the entire career. Create a separate entry for each position and for any significant change in responsibilities.
  • Returning applicants / recertification: Only add the period from the end of your last submitted work history to the present. Do not delete or rewrite old entries.
  • Include all work activities (relevant and non-relevant). Break experience into applicable technical areas with percentages and descriptions.
  • Do not copy-paste identical descriptions across different positions or periods; each description should reflect the actual work.
  • Work History accepted for Active Practitioner points may not by itself be enough for a higher-level or new-subfield application.
Official Work Experience guidance: https://www.nicet.org/work-experience

6. Submitting for recertification

  1. Ensure you have entered at least 90 CPD points for each certification you wish to renew (from two or more categories).
  2. Update Work History if you are claiming Active Practitioner points.
  3. Within roughly 6 months of expiration, a “Submit Renewal” / pay option appears on the home screen.
  4. Review your entries, then proceed to payment and checkout.
  5. NICET reviews the submission. You will be notified through the portal (approval or request for more information). Typical response window is a few weeks.

Base recertification fee and incremental fees for additional subfields are published on the Recertify page. Late/reinstatement rules apply if the certification has already expired.

7. Tips and common issues

  • Enter CPD as you go — Don’t wait until the last month. The portal stores points and documents for you.
  • Email deliverability — Whitelist nicet-noreply@useclarus.com. Check spam for password resets and portal messages.
  • Existing candidates — Never create a second profile. Use Forgot Password or contact NICET if you can’t log in.
  • Audits — Keep certificates, agendas, and employer letters. NICET may request them after you submit.
  • Multiple certifications — You need 90 points per certification/subfield. Some activities can count toward more than one if relevant.
  • Need help? Use the messaging feature in the portal when possible, or contact NICET:
    • Phone: 888-476-4238 or 703-548-1518
    • Email (evaluations): evaluations@nicet.org
    • Contact page: https://www.nicet.org/about-us/contact/
